# Data Privacy and Security Policy

## Data scope
Customer names, email addresses, and billing records are collected to provide the service. The company does not maintain a central inventory of data categories, systems, or retention periods.

## Protection
Customer database files are encrypted at rest. Connections to the customer portal use encrypted transport. Temporary exports are stored in team folders and deleted when convenient.

## Legal requirements
The company follows applicable privacy laws. No owner or process is assigned to identify changes in legal or contractual requirements.

## Incident communication
Employees report suspected exposure to the IT helpdesk. IT decides whether a customer or regulator should be contacted; no timing or approval criteria are documented.
