# Cybersecurity Risk Management Policy

## Risk objectives
The security team maintains a spreadsheet of cybersecurity risks. Each entry names a system and uses low, medium, or high labels.

## Assessment
Analysts record a short threat description and business impact. The policy does not define how likelihood is estimated or how impact and likelihood combine into a rating.

## Response
The security lead may accept, reduce, transfer, or avoid a risk. Owners and due dates are entered when known. The policy does not require risk decisions to be shared with enterprise risk management.

## Risk appetite
Leadership discusses risk tolerance during planning meetings. No approved statement, decision threshold, or review schedule is documented.
