# Information Security Policy

## Purpose and scope
This policy protects Acme Corporation information and customer records on company-managed laptops and office systems. The policy owner communicates it to employees during onboarding.

## Ownership and review
The IT manager owns this policy. The Chief Operating Officer approves it. The IT manager reviews it every year and records the approval date.

## Access control
Managers request access for team members. IT grants access needed for the employee's current work. Shared administrator accounts are permitted for urgent maintenance. Managers can ask IT to remove access when a person changes roles.

## Hardware inventory
IT keeps a spreadsheet of company laptops. The spreadsheet is updated when a laptop is issued or returned. Network equipment and cloud-hosted devices are not listed.

## Monitoring
Administrators review system logs after a security problem is reported. Network services are not continuously monitored.

## Incident handling
Employees report suspected security incidents to the IT helpdesk. The helpdesk decides whether to involve outside service providers.

## Recovery
Key systems are backed up every week. The policy does not specify who starts recovery or how restored systems are checked.

## Review note
This policy is reviewed only after a security incident. No annual review calendar is maintained.
